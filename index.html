About

The bridge between business and technical teams.

I'm a Senior Business Analyst and Process Specialist with over nine years' experience across financial services, government, consulting and enterprise transformation environments. My focus is end-to-end business analysis — process modelling in BPMN 2.0, requirements gathering, and solution design that holds up once it reaches production.

I've led AS-IS/TO-BE process mapping, written the functional specifications teams build from, and supported UAT from requirements through to sign-off — always translating between what the business needs and what the system can realistically deliver.
